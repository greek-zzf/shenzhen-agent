Went drinking 🍺 in Chegongmiao with a friend after work. Both of us felt we hadn't eaten enough, so we started the "second half".

The entrance is a small shop selling 🇰🇷 Korean goods. Walking further in, you are greeted with "Annyeonghaseyo", instantly feeling like you've arrived in Korea, hahaha. There were many 🇰🇷 Koreans seated. The service staff can understand Chinese, but I personally think they are Korean because their Chinese wasn't very fluent 😝.

Two of us ordered a **Must-Order Set for Two**: Steak Meat, Beef Brisket (Woo Samgyup), Pork Belly, Roasted Pineapple, Kimchi Pancake, Stone Pot Bibimbap, Vegetable Platter, and unlimited side dishes 🥗. Free Hanbok experience (we didn't try it). Adding to favorites on Dianping gets you a free drink 🥤.

You can choose to have the meat roasted for you and brought over, or roasted at your table. We chose the latter, hahaha, just wanted to watch the roasting process.

The Stone Pot Bibimbap and Kimchi Pancake were standard, but the 🍖 **Roast Meat** was different. It was very fragrant even eaten plain. The fat of the meat exploded 💥 in the mouth, filling it with flavor. The 🐮 **Beef Brisket** was excellent. Pick up a lettuce leaf, roll two slices of meat, add shredded radish, shredded green onion, and a little dipping sauce... wrap it up and take a big bite. Mmm~~ only hate that my balance is too low and my mouth too small.
