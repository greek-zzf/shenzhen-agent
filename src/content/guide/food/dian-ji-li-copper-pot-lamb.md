We bought a **2-person set**: 🍲 Clear Soup Pot, including half a jin (250g) of 🐮 Fatty Beef, half a jin of 🐑 Lamb Rolls, a portion of Duck Blood, Frozen Tofu, Oil Wheat Lettuce 🥬, Enoki Mushrooms, and a portion of Handmade Noodles. Giving a 5-star rating on Dianping gets you a free dish; I decisively chose the **Smashed Cucumber 🥒**—theirs is truly unique, a must-order!!!

For sauces, I suggest doing what I did: one regular scallion soy sauce, and one sesame paste.

- **Clear Soup Pot 🍲**: The soup base is excellent. After it boils, start with two bowls of seaweed soup—fresh and satisfying. The fatty beef and lamb are delicious. Coat them in a thick layer of sesame paste—firm, juicy, and rich in layers. My chopsticks couldn't stop moving.

- **Smashed Cucumber 🥒**: Especially delicious. I think it's the shop's signature. Crisp, sour, and sweet—the balance is handled perfectly, making it addictive.

- **Frozen Tofu**: After boiling, it absorbs the soup completely. The texture becomes soft like a sponge. When you eat it, the juices explode in your entire mouth. Mmm~~ (savoring).

The duck blood was tender and smooth, and the 🥬 vegetables were sweet. Unfortunately, we were too full to eat the handmade noodles, so we packed them to take home. After eating our fill, we grabbed a free "Old Ice Pop" to put a sweet period on the glorious mess we just made.
