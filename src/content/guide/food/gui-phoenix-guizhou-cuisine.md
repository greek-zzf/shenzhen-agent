Went there after work on Friday. Although the mall 🏬 was quiet, the restaurant was full of diners. We ordered a **set for 2-3 people** for about **130 RMB** on Dianping, and later added **Strange Flavor Rice (Guailu Fan)** and **Spicy Chicken Rice Noodles**. Three of us were stuffed, spending over **190 RMB** in total.

- **Potatoes 🥔**: Like the potato snacks often sold at street stalls, with some Houttuynia (Zhe'ergen) added. Tasted good 😌.

- **Strange Flavor Rice (Guailu Fan) 🍚**: Rich in ingredients and complex in texture. When it was served, it felt full of "wok hei". One bite 🍽️, and you can clearly feel the crispy sentry (pork cracklings), rice 🍚, and other chopped ingredients dancing on the tip of your tongue 👅. So fragrant! It was finished shortly after being served. Highly recommended 👍.
- **Spicy Chicken Rice Noodles 🍝**: The spicy chicken was completely different from what I've had before—unique Guizhou flavor, savory and fragrant. The rice noodles were a tiny bit 🤏 chewy. Mixed with the spicy chicken sauce 🌶️ and slurped up, carb lovers will be hugely satisfied 😌. Personally, I liked the taste 😍, while my two friends thought it was average.

- **Broth 🍲**: Both soup bases were delicious: **Sour Soup Base 🍲** and **Pickled Vegetable & Bean Base 🍲**. The Sour Soup Base received strong approval 👍 from my Guizhou friend, who exclaimed it tasted like home—I need say no more 💬. The Pickled Vegetable & Bean Base was very gentle, with soybeans 🫘, minced meat, and crispy bits at the bottom. I liked scooping a spoonful of the ingredients and soup into my bowl to drink as soup 🥣.
- **Dessert 🍮**: Taste was average, not as amazing as the other dishes.

**Summary**: Affordable price 👍, authentic taste 👅. Friends who want to experience Guizhou cuisine should definitely give it a try.
