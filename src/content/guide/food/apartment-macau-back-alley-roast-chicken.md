This is a roast chicken spot I frequently visit in Shekou. I highly recommend the **Roast Chicken (88 RMB)**—it’s a must-order ⭐️. My former landlord mentioned that the BBQ skewers are outsourced, so the quality can be inconsistent; I’d suggest ordering fewer of those.

The chicken has crispy skin and firm, tender meat, served hot straight from the hanging oven. When you take a bite, the juices from the crispy skin melt in your mouth, perfectly complementing the firm texture of the meat. It’s the kind of food that makes you forget your worries. Pure satisfaction and happiness ☺️.
