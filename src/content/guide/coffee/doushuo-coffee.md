The shop is right by the roadside, and the environment is a bit noisy. Combined with the impact of people taking photos 📷, it's hard to immerse yourself without noise-canceling headphones 🎧. There are indoor areas, an open-air small courtyard, and some window seats 💺 by the entrance—plenty of environment options to choose from.

I bought a **Tonic Coffee ☕️** for 19 RMB on Dianping, and personally I think it's quite good 😌. The taste is like coffee mixed with cola, a flavor 👅 I've never experienced before.
