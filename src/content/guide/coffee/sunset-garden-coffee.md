The coffee shop operates in a day-cafe-night-bar format, with quite a few spirits displayed on the counter. Located right by the roadside with outdoor seating as well, it's suitable for casual conversations with a few people. The interior is not large, with about 7-8 tables. The overall environment is quite noisy, not suitable for those wanting to work or read. The interior decoration is nice, with an overall color scheme of orange-red like a sunset.
