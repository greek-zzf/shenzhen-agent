My favorite coffee shop in Shenzhen (it carries some of my beautiful memories).

The coffee shop is right by the sea, with excellent scenery 👍. When you're tired of studying, you can go out for a walk and enjoy the sea breeze—absolutely delightful 😌. The shop is not large and has two floors. There's an unwritten rule in the shop: the first floor is for chatting 💬, and the second floor is for working and studying 📑.

The second floor has window seats overlooking the dock, with beautiful views. I recommend going to the second floor to experience studying while facing the sea. All seats have power outlets, suitable for bringing a laptop to work or read. Many pretty ladies also come here to take photos.

The fries 🍟 are delicious, and coffee averages around 30 RMB.
