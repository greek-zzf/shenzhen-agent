I arrived around 4:30 PM on Sunday afternoon. It was very crowded with many foreigners. The shop has 3 floors: the first floor is a free meditation 🧘 space, while the second and third floors are the business areas. The third floor has open large window seats with ocean views outside, which is particularly comfortable. But because of this, there are lots of people taking photos 📷 🤦. I've been twice and couldn't find a comfortable seat 💺 either time.

Overall, it's quite standard. The special feature is the ocean view outside the window 🪟. When tired, you can look 👀 at the sea or go downstairs to feel the sea breeze—very chill. If you can snag a window seat 💺, it's quite nice 😌. For working 💼 or studying 📑, I'd recommend Huafang Coffee instead.

The coffee ☕️ is decent. Due to the environment and location, the average price per cup is basically 30-40 RMB, which I personally find a bit expensive.
