**Decor and Environment:** Retro style. The ordering counter is on the right as you enter, with seating on the left and a row of Slam Dunk figurines. Overall quiet, with soothing light music playing in the shop. Most visitors come to work or study. There are sofa seats, private rooms, booth seating on the second floor, and warm lighting—the overall feeling is comfortable and pleasant.

**Service:** Empty plates, cups, and trash on tables are promptly cleared, and there's self-service lemon water.

**Business Hours:** Quite late. When I left at 7:30 PM, there were still many customers. I estimate they operate until around 10 PM.
