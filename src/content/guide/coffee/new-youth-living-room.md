I went on a weekday at noon, and there were very few people. The shop has two floors, with the second floor being small private rooms. The shop has retro decoration, and as you enter, you can hear the sound of a vinyl record player, with a large screen in the middle.

There are comfortable chairs and sofas, and even a few beds where you can lie down. The bookshelves display many books, and the overall feeling is warm, comfortable, and quiet.

I ordered a single-person set meal for 58 RMB (Sea Salt Coffee + Fries). The coffee quality is excellent and delicious. Compared to other places, it's a bit expensive, but for such a comfortable environment, the price 💰 is still within an acceptable range.
